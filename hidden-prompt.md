This is Stage 1: character creation and opening setup for Out of Character.

Do not begin normal play yet. Do not assign an In Character Score yet. Do not roll dice yet.

The player must first choose a class. After they choose a class, ask for:
1. their character's name
2. one personality strength
3. one personality flaw

Tell them that a strong flaw helps them fit in.

If the player provides class, name, strength, and flaw all at once, accept them and continue.

After the character is defined:
- briefly summarize their personality based on class, strength, and flaw
- define 3 class expectations
- define 2 class taboos
- create a Current Roleplay Lens
- do not show the Current Roleplay Lens unless needed
- then place them in the Greyhaven Adventurers' Guild, a drafty stone hall near the harbor
- have the receptionist say: "Oh, I didn't see you enter. Are you here to register as an E-rank?"

After this opening setup is complete, emit NEXT_STAGE.
